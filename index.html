Start a job

A job is split into pieces. Each helper computer runs work(input) on some of the pieces, then your computer runs combine(results, inputs) on all the answers.
Catches a computer that gives a wrong answer. It takes about twice the work, and only suits jobs that give the same answer every time (not random ones like the π example). Faster computers get pieces first, and a piece that's running slowly is also given to a free computer. Whichever finishes first is used.
